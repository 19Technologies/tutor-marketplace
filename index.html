Questions, answered

How much do lessons cost?

Tutors set their own rates — most charge between $10 and $35 for a 50-minute lesson. You only pay for the lessons you book, and trial lessons can be 25 or 50 minutes.

What happens in a trial lesson?

Your tutor gets to know your level and goals, then walks you through a short plan. You’ll speak a lot and leave with a clear idea of how you’d learn together.

Can I change tutors?

Any time. If your first trial isn’t a fit, your next one is free, and you can move remaining lesson hours to a new tutor.

What do I need for a lesson?

A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and a microphone. Lessons run right in the browser — nothing to install.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Reschedule or cancel for free up to 12 hours before a lesson starts, straight from the Lessons tab.
